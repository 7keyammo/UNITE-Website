Terms of service

Draft for review before launch. Have this checked by someone qualified for your situation.

Memberships

Homeschool Hub and Saturday School are subscriptions billed monthly, every six months (semester), or yearly. Saturday School includes Homeschool Hub access. Plans renew automatically until cancelled.

Cancelling

You can cancel anytime from your account. Access continues until the end of the period you have paid for.

Saturday School

Sessions run in person in Oakland. Cohorts are small, about 10 students. We confirm your cohort and start date by email after you join. A parent or guardian is responsible for drop-off and pick-up.

Content

The Wonder Ship books and other library content are for your household’s own learning. Please don’t share your login or redistribute the material.

Changes

We may update prices or these terms. We will tell members by email before a price change affects them.
