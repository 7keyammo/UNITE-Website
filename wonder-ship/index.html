How to register

Four steps to get started.

Tell us what you’re interested in, or go straight to a plan.

  1. Pick your pathHomeschool Hub, Saturday School, or a custom offering.
  2. Create your accountA parent or guardian holds the account. Members log in to reach the library.
  3. Choose a planPay monthly, by semester, or annually. Longer costs less per month.
  4. Start learningThe Hub opens right away, and we confirm your Saturday School seat by email.

Register your interest

Accounts and card payment open with the members site. Until then, email us and we’ll get you set up by hand — tell us your child’s age range and whether you want the Hub, Saturday School, or something custom.
