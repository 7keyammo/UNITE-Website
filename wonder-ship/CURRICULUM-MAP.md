# The Wonder Ship — Curriculum Crosswalk

How the two K–2 curricula are woven into a 3-year-old picture book series.

## The two source curricula

| Source | Structure | Core arc |
|---|---|---|
| **STEAM + Music: A 15-Class Curriculum for Grades K–2** | 15 × 45-min classes, zero materials | Feel sound in the body → design with it → count it → digitize it → express it → showcase |
| **Energy, Frequency & Vibration: A 15-Lesson Series for Grades K–2** | 15 × 45-min lessons, zero materials | Vibration → energy → frequency → amplitude → resonance → waves → showcase |

Both run on the same engine: **voice, clapping, stomping, humming, breath, movement, imagination. No materials.** The Wonder Ship books are the age-3 on-ramp to that engine. A child who reads all three books arrives in Kindergarten already fluent in the body vocabulary both curricula depend on.

## The translation rule

K–2 curricula **name** the concept and then explore it. Age 3 cannot hold the name. So each book **installs the body experience first** and puts the vocabulary word only in the teacher sidebar, where the adult sees it. The child does the physics; the grown-up knows what it is called.

## The 8-beat template → 24 pages

Every book is the same 24 pages. Only the world changes.

| Page | Beat | Fixed function | Curriculum thread |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | — | Title page | — |
| 2 | — | How to Fly This Book (adult note) | Facilitation |
| 3–4 | **1. Liftoff Ritual** | Breathe in, breathe out. Hand on chest. | Vibration (EFV L1) |
| 5–6 | **1. Liftoff Ritual** | Slow claps = idle. Fast claps = GO. | Energy + tempo (EFV L2, L6) |
| 7–8 | **2. Departure** | Ship rises. Porthole shows what's coming. | Anticipation, quiet |
| 9–10 | **3. Arrival** | Landing. First look at the new world. | Observation |
| 11–12 | **4. Explore A** | Discovery beat | Book-specific science |
| 13–14 | **4. Explore A** | MOVEMENT PAGE | Gross motor + concept |
| 15–16 | **5. Explore B** | Second discovery beat | Book-specific science |
| 17–18 | **5. Explore B** | MOVEMENT PAGE | Gross motor + concept |
| 19–20 | **6. Wonder Moment** | Quiet, awe. Energy/frequency/vibration payoff. | The book's big idea, felt |
| 21–22 | **7. Return Ritual** | Clap and breathe the ship home. Mirrors pp. 3–6. | Regulation / self-calming |
| 23 | **8. Landing** | Back in class. Mission Log question. | Recall + signature sound |
| 24 | — | The Wonder Ship Clap (series theme) | Pattern of 4 (SM C4) |

**Structural promise:** pages 3–6 and pages 21–22 are near-identical in every book. That repetition is the point — it is what makes the breath a tool the child can use when the book is closed.

## The series theme: The Wonder Ship Clap

Four beats. Same in all three books. Appears on page 5, page 21, and page 24.

```
   1        2        3        4
 CLAP     CLAP      PAT     WHOOSH
 hands    hands    knees    breathe out
```

This is a pattern of 4 (STEAM+Music Class 4), it is counted vibration (EFV Lesson 6, Hertz), and beat 4 is a breath — so every repetition of the theme is also a regulation cue. Slow it down to idle the ship. Speed it up to launch. That single dial is frequency (EFV Lesson 3) in a form a 3-year-old can operate.

## Teacher sidebar system

Every story page carries a footer band, always in the same place:

- **PAUSE** — the movement or breath instruction, 6 words or fewer
- **GROWN-UPS** — the curriculum link and the vocabulary word, for the adult only

The child never sees the word "amplitude." The teacher always does.

## Where this leads

The three books are the pre-K floor under both K–2 series:

- A child who has flown the Wonder Ship arrives at **STEAM+Music Class 1** already knowing a hum makes the throat shake.
- They arrive at **EFV Lesson 1** having already done the hand-on-chest hum thirty times.
- The Wonder Ship Clap is already a 4-beat pattern, so **Class 4 (Rhythm is Math)** starts from something they own.
- The breathe-in/breathe-out opener transfers directly to line-up, nap transition, and conflict de-escalation.

*Mentality Over Excuses — no materials, no excuses, all music.*
