# The Wonder Ship — Book 3: Back to the Dinosaurs

**Series:** The Wonder Ship
**Audience:** Ages 3 / Preschool / TK
**Trim:** 8.5 × 8.5 in square · 24 pages · read-aloud
**Word count:** ~240 story words
**Read-aloud time:** 6–8 minutes with movement pauses

---

## PAGE 1 — Title

> # The Wonder Ship
> ## Back to the Dinosaurs
>
> Piloted by Mr. Domkam & Mr. Johnson

---

## PAGE 2 — How to Fly This Book

> **Grown-ups, read this part first.**
>
> This book only works if you move.
>
> Every page has a **PAUSE**. Stop reading. Do the thing. Then keep going.
> The breathing on pages 3 and 4 is the same in every Wonder Ship book. That is on purpose — it becomes a tool your children can use when the book is closed.
>
> You need nothing. No materials. Just bodies, voices, and breath.

---

## PAGE 3 — Liftoff Ritual

> The Wonder Ship is sleeping.
>
> To wake her up, we breathe.
>
> Put your hand on your chest.
>
> Breathe in...

**PAUSE:** Hand on chest. Breathe in.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Vibration* — EFV Lesson 1, STEAM Class 1. Third book, same opening. By now it is theirs.

---

## PAGE 4 — Liftoff Ritual

> ...and breathe **out**.
>
> Slow. Soft. Warm.
>
> Now hum. Hmmmmmmm.
>
> Did you feel the little buzz?
>
> That is your engine.

**PAUSE:** Breathe out. Hum. Feel the buzz.
**GROWN-UPS:** Ask them where they feel it most. Throat? Lips? Chest? That is EFV Lesson 1's vibration hunt, three years early.

---

## PAGE 5 — Liftoff Ritual

> Now the claps.
>
> **Clap. Clap. Pat. Whoosh.**
>
> Slow claps keep her sleepy.
>
> Sloooow... claaap... paaat... whoooosh...

**PAUSE:** Slow it down. Four beats.
**GROWN-UPS:** Let a child lead the four beats today. They know it.

---

## PAGE 6 — Liftoff Ritual

> Faster now. Faster!
>
> Clap! Clap! Pat! Whoosh!
>
> Mr. Domkam holds on tight.
> Mr. Johnson counts.
>
> One — two — three — **GO!**

**PAUSE:** Speed up the claps until liftoff.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Energy* — EFV Lesson 2.

---

## PAGE 7 — Departure

> **UP** goes the Wonder Ship.
>
> But not up to the sky.
>
> Up... and **back**.
>
> Back and back and back.

**PAUSE:** Stretch up, then lean back. Slow.
**GROWN-UPS:** Time travel instead of space travel. Same liftoff, different direction. Ask what "back" means.

---

## PAGE 8 — Departure

> Look in the porthole.
>
> Green.
>
> So much green.
>
> And something is **moving**.

**PAUSE:** Make a porthole with your hands. Peek through.
**GROWN-UPS:** Quiet beat. Three seconds.

---

## PAGE 9 — Arrival

> Down, down, down.
>
> Leaves brush the glass.
>
> The Wonder Ship lands in the tall, tall grass.
>
> *crunch.*

**PAUSE:** Wiggle your fingers like tall grass.
**GROWN-UPS:** Whisper "crunch." The jungle is loud, but you arrive quiet.

---

## PAGE 10 — Arrival

> We are with the dinosaurs.
>
> Open your eyes big.
>
> What do you see?

**PAUSE:** Open your eyes wide. Look around.
**GROWN-UPS:** Take every answer.

---

## PAGE 11 — Explore A · Discovery

> Put your hands flat on the ground.
>
> Wait.
>
> .
>
> .
>
> Do you feel that?

**PAUSE:** Both palms flat on the floor. Wait. Be still.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Energy transfer through solids* — EFV Lesson 2 and Lesson 11. Sound travels through the floor faster than through the air.

---

## PAGE 12 — Explore A · Discovery

> **BOOM. BOOM. BOOM.**
>
> Something big is coming.
>
> The ground is talking
>
> to your hands.

**PAUSE:** Keep hands down. Feel three booms.
**GROWN-UPS:** "The ground is talking to your hands" is your reusable line for vibration all year.

---

## PAGE 13 — Explore A · MOVEMENT

> Stand up!
>
> **STOMP!**
>
> BOOM. BOOM. BOOM. BOOM.
>
> Count them:
>
> one, two, three, four.

**PAUSE:** Four big stomps. Count out loud.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Counting vibrations* — EFV Lesson 6, STEAM Class 4. Four beats, same as the Wonder Ship Clap.

---

## PAGE 14 — Explore A · MOVEMENT

> Now stomp **twice as fast**!
>
> boom-boom-boom-boom-boom-boom-boom-boom
>
> Whee!
>
> Now slow.
>
> Big and slow again.

**PAUSE:** Eight fast stomps, then four slow.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Doubling* — EFV Lesson 10. Four became eight. They will meet this again as octaves.

---

## PAGE 15 — Explore B · Discovery

> Here is the **biggest** dinosaur.
>
> Her step is huge.
>
> **BOOOOOM.**

**PAUSE:** One giant stomp. As big as you can.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Amplitude* — EFV Lesson 4. Big vibration.

---

## PAGE 16 — Explore B · Discovery

> Here is the **littlest** dinosaur.
>
> His step is tiny.
>
> *tip. tip. tip.*
>
> Same ground. Different size.

**PAUSE:** Six tiny tiptoes.
**GROWN-UPS:** Same ground, same feet, different size of vibration. That is amplitude, separate from speed.

---

## PAGE 17 — Explore B · MOVEMENT

> Be the big one.
>
> **BIG STOMP!**
>
> Be the little one.
>
> *tiny tiptoe.*
>
> Big. Tiny. Big. Tiny.

**PAUSE:** Alternate four times.
**GROWN-UPS:** Force grading. Hardest skill on this page is the *tiny* one.

---

## PAGE 18 — Explore B · MOVEMENT

> Now **roar**!
>
> A big roar is low —
>
> **RRRRAAAWWWR.**
>
> A little roar is high —
>
> *eek! eek!*
>
> Try both.

**PAUSE:** One low roar. Three high squeaks.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Frequency* again — EFV Lesson 3. Big animal, low sound. They already believe this; now they have done it.

---

## PAGE 19 — Wonder Moment

> Mr. Domkam cups his hands
>
> and calls to the mountain:
>
> **HEL-LOOOO!**
>
> Wait.
>
> *hel-loooo... hel-loooo... hel-loooo*

**PAUSE:** Cup your hands. Call out. Then wait and listen.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Acoustics and echo* — STEAM Class 9. The wait is the magic. Do not talk into it.

---

## PAGE 20 — Wonder Moment

> The mountain caught his voice
>
> and threw it back.
>
> Try it.
>
> Say your name to the mountain.
>
> It knows your name now.

**PAUSE:** Each child calls their own name. Everyone echoes it back softly.
**GROWN-UPS:** The class becomes the mountain. Echo each name three times, quieter each time. This is the emotional peak of the book.

---

## PAGE 21 — Return Ritual

> Time to go home.
>
> Hand on your chest.
>
> Breathe in...
>
> ...and breathe out.

**PAUSE:** Same breath as page 3. Twice.
**GROWN-UPS:** The mirror. Same in all three books.

---

## PAGE 22 — Return Ritual

> Clap. Clap. Pat. Whoosh.
>
> Faster! Faster!
>
> Forward through the green.
>
> Home, home, **HOME!**

**PAUSE:** The Wonder Ship Clap. Slow to fast.
**GROWN-UPS:** Book 1 went up, Book 2 came down, Book 3 goes forward. Three directions, one clap.

---

## PAGE 23 — Landing

> *Bump.*
>
> We are back.
>
> Mr. Domkam laughs.
> Mr. Johnson claps once.
>
> **MISSION LOG**
> What did the mountain say back to you?

**PAUSE:** Everyone makes their own sound. Just one.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Signature sound* — STEAM Class 12. Name it: "That one is yours. Nobody else makes that."

---

## PAGE 24 — The Wonder Ship Clap

> ## The Wonder Ship Clap
>
> You can fly any time.
>
> **1. CLAP** your hands
> **2. CLAP** your hands
> **3. PAT** your knees
> **4. WHOOSH** — breathe out
>
> Slow to rest.
> Fast to fly.
>
> *Mentality Over Excuses — no materials, no excuses, all music.*
