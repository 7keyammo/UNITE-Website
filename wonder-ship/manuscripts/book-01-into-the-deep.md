# The Wonder Ship — Book 1: Into the Deep

**Series:** The Wonder Ship
**Audience:** Ages 3 / Preschool / TK
**Trim:** 8.5 × 8.5 in square · 24 pages · read-aloud
**Word count:** ~230 story words
**Read-aloud time:** 6–8 minutes with movement pauses

---

## PAGE 1 — Title

> # The Wonder Ship
> ## Into the Deep
>
> Piloted by Mr. Domkam & Mr. Johnson

---

## PAGE 2 — How to Fly This Book

> **Grown-ups, read this part first.**
>
> This book only works if you move.
>
> Every page has a **PAUSE**. Stop reading. Do the thing. Then keep going.
> The breathing on pages 3 and 4 is the same every time, in every book. That is on purpose — it becomes a tool your children can use when the book is closed.
>
> You need nothing. No materials. Just bodies, voices, and breath.

---

## PAGE 3 — Liftoff Ritual

> The Wonder Ship is sleeping.
>
> To wake her up, we breathe.
>
> Put your hand on your chest.
>
> Breathe in...

**PAUSE:** Hand on chest. Breathe in.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Vibration* — EFV Lesson 1, STEAM Class 1. Hands stay on chests for the whole spread.

---

## PAGE 4 — Liftoff Ritual

> ...and breathe **out**.
>
> Slow. Soft. Warm.
>
> Now hum. Hmmmmmmm.
>
> Did you feel the little buzz?
>
> That is your engine.

**PAUSE:** Breathe out. Hum. Feel the buzz.
**GROWN-UPS:** Every sound starts as a vibration. They just found theirs. *Vibration.*

---

## PAGE 5 — Liftoff Ritual

> Now the claps.
>
> **Clap. Clap. Pat. Whoosh.**
>
> Slow claps keep her sleepy.
>
> Sloooow... claaap... paaat... whoooosh...

**PAUSE:** Slow it down. Four beats.
**GROWN-UPS:** The Wonder Ship Clap. A pattern of 4 — STEAM Class 4. Slow tempo = low energy.

---

## PAGE 6 — Liftoff Ritual

> Faster now. Faster!
>
> Clap! Clap! Pat! Whoosh!
>
> Mr. Domkam grins. Mr. Johnson counts.
>
> One — two — three — **GO!**

**PAUSE:** Speed up the claps until liftoff.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Energy* — EFV Lesson 2. Harder, faster claps carry more energy. The children are the engine.

---

## PAGE 7 — Departure

> **UP** goes the Wonder Ship.
>
> Up past the window.
>
> Up past the birds.
>
> Up past the clouds.

**PAUSE:** Stretch both arms up. Reach.
**GROWN-UPS:** Big stretch resets the body after the fast claps.

---

## PAGE 8 — Departure

> Look in the porthole.
>
> Blue.
>
> All blue.
>
> Something big and blue is coming.

**PAUSE:** Make a porthole with your hands. Peek through.
**GROWN-UPS:** Quiet beat. Let the anticipation sit for three seconds before you turn.

---

## PAGE 9 — Arrival

> Down, down, down.
>
> Bubbles tickle the glass.
>
> The Wonder Ship lands soft on the sand.
>
> *bloop... bloop... bloop*

**PAUSE:** Wiggle your fingers like bubbles going up.
**GROWN-UPS:** *bloop* is your cue to drop your voice to a whisper. Underwater is a quiet world.

---

## PAGE 10 — Arrival

> We are under the sea.
>
> Open your eyes big.
>
> What do you see?

**PAUSE:** Open your eyes wide. Look around.
**GROWN-UPS:** Take answers. Every answer is right. This is observation, the first science skill.

---

## PAGE 11 — Explore A · Discovery

> Put your hands behind your ears.
>
> Cup them. Like this.
>
> Listen.

**PAUSE:** Cup your hands behind your ears.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Your amazing ears* — STEAM Class 7. The outer ear is a dish. Bigger dish, more sound.

---

## PAGE 12 — Explore A · Discovery

> Down here, sound is slow and sleepy.
>
> Everything sounds far away.
>
> A little fish goes by.
>
> No footsteps. No stomping.
>
> Just... *shhhhhh.*

**PAUSE:** Say *shhhhh* very softly.
**GROWN-UPS:** Water carries sound differently than air. Muffled, heavy, slow. *Medium* — EFV Lesson 11.

---

## PAGE 13 — Explore A · MOVEMENT

> Now **swim**.
>
> Make a wave with your arm.
>
> Up... and down...
>
> and up... and down...

**PAUSE:** Stand up. Wave one arm like a wave.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Waves* — EFV Lesson 13. They are drawing a waveform in the air without knowing it.

---

## PAGE 14 — Explore A · MOVEMENT

> That is how sound moves.
>
> Wave. Wave. Wave.
>
> Swim it slow.
>
> Swim it with two arms.
>
> Swim it with your whole body!

**PAUSE:** Whole-body wave. Slow.
**GROWN-UPS:** Slow, big waves = low and loud. Say it if you like; they will meet the words again in Kindergarten.

---

## PAGE 15 — Explore B · Discovery

> **CLICK!**
>
> A tiny shrimp snaps his claw.
>
> Such a big sound
>
> from such a little friend.

**PAUSE:** Snap or clap once. Loud!
**GROWN-UPS:** *Loud and soft* — STEAM Class 2. Small body, big energy.

---

## PAGE 16 — Explore B · Discovery

> Then he hides in the soft, soft sand.
>
> Click...
>
> Now it is tiny.
>
> Soft things eat sound.

**PAUSE:** Clap once with soft, floppy hands.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Absorption* — EFV Lesson 12, STEAM Class 14. Soft material soaks up sound energy.

---

## PAGE 17 — Explore B · MOVEMENT

> Your turn!
>
> **BIG** clap — BOOM!
>
> **tiny** clap — soft.
>
> Big. Tiny. Big. Tiny.

**PAUSE:** Four claps. Big, tiny, big, tiny.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Amplitude* — EFV Lesson 4. Same hands. Different size of vibration.

---

## PAGE 18 — Explore B · MOVEMENT

> Now stomp like a big whale tail.
>
> **BOOM!**
>
> Now tiptoe like a seahorse.
>
> *tip. tip. tip.*
>
> Same feet. Different size.

**PAUSE:** Two big stomps. Six tiny tiptoes.
**GROWN-UPS:** "Same feet, different size" is amplitude in five words. Reuse that line all year.

---

## PAGE 19 — Wonder Moment

> Then...
>
> everything
>
> goes
>
> still.
>
> **Hoooooooooo.**
>
> A whale is singing.

**PAUSE:** Nobody moves. Just listen.
**GROWN-UPS:** Hold this silence for a full five seconds. Do not rush it. This is the heart of the book.

---

## PAGE 20 — Wonder Moment

> Put your hand on your chest.
>
> Hum with her.
>
> **Hoooooooooo.**
>
> Feel that?
>
> Her song is inside you now.

**PAUSE:** Hand on chest. Hum low and long with the whale.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Resonance* — EFV Lesson 9. One vibration makes another thing vibrate too. Their chest is the second thing.

---

## PAGE 21 — Return Ritual

> Time to go home.
>
> Hand on your chest.
>
> Breathe in...
>
> ...and breathe out.

**PAUSE:** Same breath as page 3. Twice.
**GROWN-UPS:** The mirror. This is the regulation tool — the exact move you will use at line-up and nap time.

---

## PAGE 22 — Return Ritual

> Clap. Clap. Pat. Whoosh.
>
> Faster! Faster!
>
> Up through the blue.
>
> Up, up, **UP!**

**PAUSE:** The Wonder Ship Clap. Slow to fast.
**GROWN-UPS:** Same four beats as page 5. Third time today. That is how a pattern becomes theirs.

---

## PAGE 23 — Landing

> *Bump.*
>
> We are back.
>
> Mr. Domkam smiles.
> Mr. Johnson claps once.
>
> **MISSION LOG**
> What did the whale feel like inside you?

**PAUSE:** Everyone makes their own sound. Just one.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Signature sound* — STEAM Class 12. Every child's sound is different. Say so out loud.

---

## PAGE 24 — The Wonder Ship Clap

> ## The Wonder Ship Clap
>
> You can fly any time.
>
> **1. CLAP** your hands
> **2. CLAP** your hands
> **3. PAT** your knees
> **4. WHOOSH** — breathe out
>
> Slow to rest.
> Fast to fly.
>
> *Mentality Over Excuses — no materials, no excuses, all music.*
