# The Wonder Ship — Book 2: Among the Stars

**Series:** The Wonder Ship
**Audience:** Ages 3 / Preschool / TK
**Trim:** 8.5 × 8.5 in square · 24 pages · read-aloud
**Word count:** ~235 story words
**Read-aloud time:** 6–8 minutes with movement pauses

---

## PAGE 1 — Title

> # The Wonder Ship
> ## Among the Stars
>
> Piloted by Mr. Domkam & Mr. Johnson

---

## PAGE 2 — How to Fly This Book

> **Grown-ups, read this part first.**
>
> This book only works if you move.
>
> Every page has a **PAUSE**. Stop reading. Do the thing. Then keep going.
> The breathing on pages 3 and 4 is the same in every Wonder Ship book. That is on purpose — it becomes a tool your children can use when the book is closed.
>
> You need nothing. No materials. Just bodies, voices, and breath.

---

## PAGE 3 — Liftoff Ritual

> The Wonder Ship is sleeping.
>
> To wake her up, we breathe.
>
> Put your hand on your chest.
>
> Breathe in...

**PAUSE:** Hand on chest. Breathe in.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Vibration* — EFV Lesson 1, STEAM Class 1. Identical to Book 1. The sameness is the lesson.

---

## PAGE 4 — Liftoff Ritual

> ...and breathe **out**.
>
> Slow. Soft. Warm.
>
> Now hum. Hmmmmmmm.
>
> Did you feel the little buzz?
>
> That is your engine.

**PAUSE:** Breathe out. Hum. Feel the buzz.
**GROWN-UPS:** By Book 2 they should hum before you ask. That is fluency.

---

## PAGE 5 — Liftoff Ritual

> Now the claps.
>
> **Clap. Clap. Pat. Whoosh.**
>
> Slow claps keep her sleepy.
>
> Sloooow... claaap... paaat... whoooosh...

**PAUSE:** Slow it down. Four beats.
**GROWN-UPS:** The Wonder Ship Clap — a pattern of 4, STEAM Class 4.

---

## PAGE 6 — Liftoff Ritual

> Faster now. Faster!
>
> Clap! Clap! Pat! Whoosh!
>
> Mr. Johnson checks the sky.
> Mr. Domkam counts.
>
> One — two — three — **GO!**

**PAUSE:** Speed up the claps until liftoff.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Energy* — EFV Lesson 2. Faster claps, more energy, more lift.

---

## PAGE 7 — Departure

> **UP** goes the Wonder Ship.
>
> Up past the roof.
>
> Up past the birds.
>
> Up past the clouds.
>
> Up past the sky.

**PAUSE:** Stretch both arms up. Reach higher on each "up."
**GROWN-UPS:** Five ups, five reaches. Build the stretch.

---

## PAGE 8 — Departure

> Look in the porthole.
>
> Black.
>
> All black.
>
> And... sparkles.

**PAUSE:** Make a porthole with your hands. Peek through.
**GROWN-UPS:** Quiet beat. Three seconds before turning.

---

## PAGE 9 — Arrival

> The engine goes quiet.
>
> No bump.
>
> No landing.
>
> We just... float.

**PAUSE:** Go completely still. Freeze.
**GROWN-UPS:** Stillness after motion. Notice who can hold it — that is self-regulation developing.

---

## PAGE 10 — Arrival

> We are in space.
>
> Open your eyes big.
>
> What do you see?

**PAUSE:** Open your eyes wide. Look around.
**GROWN-UPS:** Take every answer. Observation before explanation.

---

## PAGE 11 — Explore A · Discovery

> Look! A tiny, tiny star.
>
> Tiny stars sing **high**.
>
> *eee — eee — eee*

**PAUSE:** Sing high with the tiny star. *eee!*
**GROWN-UPS:** *Frequency* — EFV Lesson 3. Fast vibration sounds high.

---

## PAGE 12 — Explore A · Discovery

> Look! A big, big star.
>
> Big stars sing **low**.
>
> *ohhhhhhhhhh*

**PAUSE:** Sing low with the big star. *ohhh.*
**GROWN-UPS:** Slow vibration sounds low. Same voice, different speed. That is the whole idea.

---

## PAGE 13 — Explore A · MOVEMENT

> Reach up high — sing high!
>
> *eee!*
>
> Curl down low — sing low!
>
> *ohhh.*
>
> High. Low. High. Low.

**PAUSE:** Four times. Reach and curl.
**GROWN-UPS:** Body height mapped to pitch. Pure EFV Lesson 3, and it works on 3-year-olds.

---

## PAGE 14 — Explore A · MOVEMENT

> Now wiggle your hands fast.
>
> Fast wiggle — high sound!
>
> Now wave them slow.
>
> Slow wave — low sound!
>
> Your body is the dial.

**PAUSE:** Wiggle fast = high. Wave slow = low.
**GROWN-UPS:** They are operating a frequency control. Hold your own hand up and conduct them.

---

## PAGE 15 — Explore B · Discovery

> Step out. Careful!
>
> There is no **down** here.
>
> You float.

**PAUSE:** Take one very slow step.
**GROWN-UPS:** Slow-motion movement is hard. It builds control, not just energy release.

---

## PAGE 16 — Explore B · Discovery

> Mr. Johnson floats upside down.
>
> Mr. Domkam floats in a slow, slow circle.
>
> Float with them.

**PAUSE:** Arms wide. Turn slowly in one circle.
**GROWN-UPS:** Vestibular input. One circle only, then stop and steady.

---

## PAGE 17 — Explore B · MOVEMENT

> **Float!**
>
> Arms wide. Sloooow.
>
> One... slow... step.
>
> Two... slow... step.
>
> Three... slow... step.

**PAUSE:** Three slow floating steps.
**GROWN-UPS:** Counting to three while moving. Numeracy hidden inside gross motor.

---

## PAGE 18 — Explore B · MOVEMENT

> Now a **moon jump**!
>
> Bend down low...
>
> and... **BOING!**
>
> You go so high.
>
> You come down so *slow*.

**PAUSE:** Three moon jumps. Land soft.
**GROWN-UPS:** Big proprioceptive input. This is the energy-release page — spend time here.

---

## PAGE 19 — Wonder Moment

> Now stop.
>
> Listen.
>
> .
>
> .
>
> .
>
> **Nothing.**

**PAUSE:** Total silence. Five seconds.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Vacuum* — EFV Lesson 11, STEAM Class 11. Space is empty. Sound has nothing to travel through. Do not fill this page with words.

---

## PAGE 20 — Wonder Moment

> Space is empty.
>
> Sound needs something to swim through.
>
> But hum — **hmmmmm** —
>
> and you hear it.
>
> Because **you** are something.

**PAUSE:** Hand on chest. Hum. You can hear yourself.
**GROWN-UPS:** Sound travels through solids — including the child's own bones. It is the one sound that works in space, and they carry it. Big idea, small words.

---

## PAGE 21 — Return Ritual

> Time to go home.
>
> Hand on your chest.
>
> Breathe in...
>
> ...and breathe out.

**PAUSE:** Same breath as page 3. Twice.
**GROWN-UPS:** The mirror. Same in all three books.

---

## PAGE 22 — Return Ritual

> Clap. Clap. Pat. Whoosh.
>
> Faster! Faster!
>
> Down through the sparkles.
>
> Down, down, **DOWN!**

**PAUSE:** The Wonder Ship Clap. Slow to fast.
**GROWN-UPS:** Note the flip — Book 1 goes UP to come home, Book 2 comes DOWN. Ask them why.

---

## PAGE 23 — Landing

> *Bump.*
>
> We are back.
>
> Mr. Johnson smiles.
> Mr. Domkam claps once.
>
> **MISSION LOG**
> What did the quiet in space sound like?

**PAUSE:** Everyone makes their own sound. Just one.
**GROWN-UPS:** *Signature sound* — STEAM Class 12. Different every child, every time.

---

## PAGE 24 — The Wonder Ship Clap

> ## The Wonder Ship Clap
>
> You can fly any time.
>
> **1. CLAP** your hands
> **2. CLAP** your hands
> **3. PAT** your knees
> **4. WHOOSH** — breathe out
>
> Slow to rest.
> Fast to fly.
>
> *Mentality Over Excuses — no materials, no excuses, all music.*
