# The Wonder Ship — Teacher & Facilitator Guide

For preschool and TK teachers, and for grown-ups reading at home. Nothing here requires materials.

---

## What this series is

Three read-aloud books for 3-year-olds that are also the pre-K on-ramp to two K–2 curricula:

- **STEAM + Music** — 15 classes, Grades K–2
- **Energy, Frequency & Vibration** — 15 lessons, Grades K–2

Both K–2 series run on voice, clapping, stomping, humming, breath, and movement. The Wonder Ship installs that vocabulary in the body three years early, as a story instead of a lesson.

---

## How to read one

**Total time:** 12–18 minutes with all the pauses. Do not skip the pauses — they are the curriculum.

1. **Sit low.** Children on the floor, you at their level.
2. **Read page 2 to yourself, not out loud.** It is for you.
3. **Do every PAUSE.** Read the story text, then stop, do the action with them, then turn.
4. **Hold the silences.** Pages 19 of every book have a written silence. Count five in your head. It will feel long. That is correct.
5. **Take every answer** on the "What do you see?" page. There are no wrong ones.
6. **End with the Mission Log** on page 23. One question, one round of answers.

---

## The three things that repeat

These are the same in all three books. That is the entire design.

### 1. The Breath (pages 3–4, 21)
Hand on chest. Breathe in. Breathe out. Hum.

**Use this outside the book.** Line-up. Transition to nap. A child who is escalating. Once the children associate the move with the ship, you can say "hand on your chest, wake up the ship" anywhere in the day and get a regulated breath out of it.

### 2. The Wonder Ship Clap (pages 5–6, 22, 24)

```
   1        2        3        4
 CLAP     CLAP      PAT     WHOOSH
 hands    hands    knees   breathe out
```

Slow it to settle the room. Speed it to raise energy. Beat 4 is always a breath, so even the fast version ends in an exhale.

**Use this outside the book** as your attention signal. You clap the four beats; they clap them back.

### 3. The Signature Sound (page 23)
Every child makes one sound that is theirs. Different every time is fine. Name it out loud: *"That one is yours. Nobody else makes that one."*

## Reading order

Read them in order the first time. Each book assumes the ritual from the one before.

| Book | World | The big feeling |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Into the Deep | Underwater | Sound can be soft, slow, and muffled |
| 2 · Among the Stars | Space | Sound can be *absent* — and you still carry your own |
| 3 · Back to the Dinosaurs | Prehistoric | Sound can be enormous, and the world answers back |

After that, read them in any order, as often as they ask.

## If you have a mixed-age group

**With 4s and 5s in the room:** add the vocabulary word out loud. Ask "why do you think the big star sings low?" The older children will theorize; the 3-year-olds will just do the movement. Both are the right response.

**With K–2 children:** use the books as the warm-up for the matching lesson. Book 1 pages 19–20 is a five-minute entry into EFV Lesson 9 (Resonance). Book 2 page 19 is the entry into EFV Lesson 11 (Waves) and STEAM Class 11.

---

## Behavior notes

- **The moon jumps (Book 2, page 18) are the loudest moment in the series.** Put them where you have room, and know that page 19's silence immediately follows. That contrast is deliberate and it works.
- **Some children will not hum in a group** for the first several readings. Do not require it. Hand on the chest is enough; they will hum when they are ready.
- **The echo page (Book 3, page 20) needs turn-taking.** Go around a circle. Each child calls their name, everyone echoes it back three times, getting quieter. Takes about four minutes for a group of twelve and it is worth all four.

## Extending past the books

Once all three are familiar:

1. **Invent a fourth destination together.** Where should the Wonder Ship go? Keep the same eight beats: breathe, clap, lift off, arrive, explore, move, wonder, come home. The children can build the whole story if you hold the frame.
2. **Move up to the K–2 series.** *STEAM + Music* Class 1 and *Energy, Frequency & Vibration* Lesson 1 both open with a hand on the throat or chest and a hum — exactly where the Wonder Ship starts. The children will already know the answer.
3. **Run a Wonder Ship showcase for families.** Both K–2 series end in a showcase. Yours can too: read Book 3 aloud with the children performing every PAUSE. No materials, no rehearsal set, nothing to buy.
