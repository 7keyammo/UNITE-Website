# The Wonder Ship — Illustration Brief

For the illustrator. Read the whole thing before drawing anything.

## The one-sentence brief

Bright, high-contrast, simple shapes. A 3-year-old should be able to name every object on every page in one word.

---

## Non-negotiables

1. **Characters look identical across all three books.** Same proportions, same colors, same clothes. A child must recognize Mr. Domkam on page 3 of Book 3 as the same person from Book 1.
2. **Every spread leaves a clear text zone** — see *Layout grid* below. Never put busy detail under type.
3. **The children in the illustrations do the movement described on that page.** If the text says stomp, the drawn children are stomping. The art is the demonstration.
4. **Backgrounds go quiet on the Wonder Moment pages (19–20).** Fewer objects, more empty space, deeper color. The reader should feel the room get still.
5. **Diverse classroom, always.** Skin tones, hair textures, body sizes, at least one child using mobility equipment across the series. Never tokenized to a single page.

---

## Character sheets

### Mr. Domkam
- Warm, tall, round-shouldered. The one who grins.
- **Palette:** deep teal jacket, mustard scarf, brown boots.
- **Signature gesture:** hands cupped around his mouth (he is the one who calls to the mountain in Book 3).
- Always the character closest to the children in a group shot.

### Mr. Johnson
- Compact, energetic, glasses. The one who counts.
- **Palette:** rust-orange vest, white shirt, teal sneakers (deliberate echo of Domkam's teal).
- **Signature gesture:** one finger up, counting the beat.
- Always the character checking the instruments/sky/ground.

> **Name note:** the brief uses **Mr. Domkam** throughout. The original planning doc spelled it "Mr. Domcom" once. Confirm the correct spelling before final art — it appears on every title page.

### The Wonder Ship
- A round, friendly vessel — **not** a rocket. Think a wooden reading-nook boat crossed with a hot-air-balloon basket.
- **One big porthole**, front and center. The porthole is a recurring compositional device: pages 8 and 9 of every book frame the new world inside it.
- **Visible "breath lights"** — a ring of soft glowing dots around the hull that pulse with the breathing. Dim on page 3, brighter on page 4, full glow by page 6. This is the reader's visual feedback that their breath is doing something.
- **A clap-meter** — four lamps in a row on the console, lighting one per beat of the Wonder Ship Clap. On page 5 they light slowly; page 6 they blink fast.
- Interior is warm wood and cushions. It should look like somewhere you would want to sit.

### The children
- Four to six recurring kids, ages 3–5. Give them consistent hair and clothing so children can find "their" kid across the series.

## Palette

| Book | Dominant | Accent | Wonder Moment shift |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Into the Deep | Deep teal → aqua | Coral, warm sand | Page 19–20 goes near-navy with one warm whale silhouette |
| 2 · Among the Stars | Indigo → violet | Gold, white sparkle | Page 19 is the darkest page in the series — almost empty |
| 3 · Back to the Dinosaurs | Jungle green → olive | Warm ochre, dusty rose | Page 19–20 opens into a wide pale canyon, lots of sky |

Keep the ship's teal/mustard/rust palette constant against all three worlds so it always reads as "ours."

## Layout grid (8.5 × 8.5 in square)

```
┌─────────────────────────────┐  ← 0.125" bleed all round
│                             │
│                             │
│        ILLUSTRATION         │  full-bleed art
│           ZONE              │
│                             │
│   ┌─────────────────────┐   │
│   │    STORY TEXT       │   │  ← keep art calm behind this
│   └─────────────────────┘   │
│ ┌─────────────────────────┐ │
│ │ PAUSE / GROWN-UPS BAND  │ │  ← solid color band, art never enters
│ └─────────────────────────┘ │
└─────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Trim:** 8.5 × 8.5 in
- **Bleed:** 0.125 in on all four sides → build at 8.75 × 8.75 in
- **Safe margin:** 0.5 in from trim for all text
- **Gutter allowance:** 0.375 in on the inner edge for perfect-bound interiors
- **Resolution:** 300 dpi minimum, CMYK for print export, sRGB for the ebook version

The **PAUSE / GROWN-UPS band** is a solid color bar across the bottom. It is the same height and position on every page of every book. Art never bleeds into it. This is what makes the template reusable — Books 2 and 3 drop new art into an identical frame.

## Style references to avoid

- No photorealism.
- No thin outlines — a 3-year-old at arm's length needs weight.
- No small background gags. This is not a seek-and-find book; the movement is the activity.
- No text baked into the artwork. All type lives in the layout so the books can be translated and reskinned.
